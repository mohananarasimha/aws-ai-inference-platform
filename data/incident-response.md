# Incident Response Policy

An incident should be declared when a production issue causes significant customer impact, sustained elevated error rates, or major service degradation.

The on-call engineer becomes the initial incident commander until responsibility is transferred.

During an incident, responders should prioritize service restoration over identifying the complete root cause.

Important actions, observations, and decisions must be recorded in the incident channel.

After service recovery, a postmortem should document the timeline, root cause, customer impact, remediation, and preventive actions.

Blameless postmortems should focus on system and process improvements rather than individual mistakes.
